Alongside the bigger releases, a long list of things that now simply work the way you'd expect.

## Charts

- **Bar charts handle negative values.** The scale always includes zero and bars grow from the zero line in both orientations — profit and loss charts now just work.
- **Stacked horizontal bars** draw every segment at the right width and grow from their own position instead of overlapping while they animate.
- **Line charts morph** smoothly when their data changes, rather than snapping to the new values.
- **Markers and edge points** are no longer clipped at the edge of the plot.
- **Ring charts** no longer flicker when you hover near the inner edge of a ring.
- **Custom `PieCenter` content** shows at rest, not only while hovering a slice.
- **Persian, Arabic and Hebrew labels** animate as a single run so letters stay joined and in order, and the date ticker only scrolls the part of the date that changed.

## Installing with shadcn

- Charts installed from the registry keep their imports valid in your project's folder layout.
- Theme tokens map correctly into your `@theme` variables.
- Every chart passes the React Compiler lint rules.
