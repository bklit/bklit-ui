Bklit UI has a new home. The site, docs and Studio now use [coss ui](https://coss.com/ui) components and its colour palette in place of our shadcn primitives.

## Frames and rails

Pages sit between two thin rails that now run all the way up through the header, with small square rivets where section dividers meet them. Docs pages sit in frames on a tinted canvas, and every component has its own illustration.

## Cal Sans

Headings and body text now use Cal Sans, the same typeface as coss ui, for a tighter and friendlier voice.

## A simpler homepage

The homepage hero has been pared back, and the community showcase has moved to its own [Showcase](/showcase) page.

Studio keeps its square, tool-like chrome. The navigation and every URL stay the same.
