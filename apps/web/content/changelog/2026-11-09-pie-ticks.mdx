A donut doesn't have to be solid. `PieTicks` draws a pie chart as a ring of radial ticks. Use it inside `PieChart` in place of `PieSlice`, and each slice gets a share of the ticks in proportion to its value, with an empty slot between slices.

## Fills like a gauge

On mount the ticks light up one after another around the ring, like a gauge filling. One motion value drives every tick, so the sweep doesn't re-render React on each frame. `count` sets how many ticks share the ring (96 by default), and `strokeWidth`, `lineCap` and `sliceGap` shape them.

## Two ways to hover

The default, `hoverEffect="slice"`, extends every tick in the hovered slice and dims the others. `hoverEffect="tick"` shoots out only the tick under the pointer and lifts its neighbours a little. Ticks spring out quickly and settle slowly, so sweeping across the ring leaves a trail. The hovered slice still drives `PieCenter`.

## A percent readout

`PieCenter` has a new `valueDisplay="percent"` that shows the hovered slice's share of the total, and 100% at rest. It turns a tick ring into a gauge-style readout.

Try the [Tick ring recipe](/recipes#tick-ring), or pick **Ticks** under Style in [Studio](/studio?chart=pie-chart). See [PieTicks in the pie chart docs](/docs/components/pie-chart).
