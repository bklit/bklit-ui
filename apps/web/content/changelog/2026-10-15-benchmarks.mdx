Performance claims are easy to make and hard to check. [Benchmarks](/benchmarks) lets you check ours: it runs Bklit UI, Recharts and TanStack Charts against the same seeded data — line, area, bar and scatter — in your own browser.

## What it measures

Each run reports how long a chart takes to mount, to update and to respond to hover, how many DOM nodes it creates, and how much it adds to your bundle, then combines them into a score. Enter animations are timed for reference but never scored, so no library is penalised for animating.

## Smaller tooltip dates

Building it, we also made the tooltip's date pill fit its label: it measures the current date and springs its width as you move, instead of sizing itself to the longest month.
