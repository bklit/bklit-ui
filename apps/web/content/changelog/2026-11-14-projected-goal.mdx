"Will we hit $10K this month?" is a question a chart should answer at a glance. `ProjectionLine` can now fill the area under the forecast and mark the moment it crosses your goal.

## A fill for the forecast

Set `areaFill` to `"solid"`, `"gradient"` or `"pattern"` to fill under the projection, separately from the actual data. Tune it with `areaColor`, `areaOpacity` and `areaFadeBottom`. With a pattern, `areaPattern` picks any of the pattern presets and `areaPatternTint` adds a gradient wash beneath the hatch.

## Goal crossing

Pass a `target` and the chart draws a dashed goal line, plus a glowing ring where the projection crosses it. This works for straight and curved projections. `showGuides` drops dashed verticals from the hand-off point and the crossing. `showEndLabel` and `formatEndLabel` label the last projected value, and `endMarkerFill` makes the end dot hollow.

The new **Projected goal** recipe projects month-to-date revenue past a $10K goal, in area and line variants. There's also a **Projection Area** example on the [area chart page](/charts/area-chart), and every projection in [Studio](/studio?chart=line-chart) gets Area, Goal, Guides and End label controls.
