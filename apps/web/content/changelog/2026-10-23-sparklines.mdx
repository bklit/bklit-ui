A KPI card needs a trend, not a chart: no axes, no grid, no tooltip, and it has to stay cheap when there are fifty of them on the page. `LineSparkline`, `AreaSparkline` and `BarSparkline` are built for exactly that. Pass `data` and a `dataKey` and they fill their parent at 32px tall.

## Built for density

Each sparkline renders a single `<path>`, bars included. Every responsive sparkline on the page shares one `ResizeObserver`, and passing a fixed `width` skips measuring entirely. Only the hovered instance re-renders, so a grid of 200 stays smooth.

## Enter animations, on by default

Animations run on the Web Animations API instead of React state. Lines draw in along their stroke, areas wipe in and bars grow from the baseline. Tune them with `animationDuration` and `animationEasing`, or pass `animate={false}`. With reduced motion turned on, each one becomes a short fade.

## Hover without a tooltip

A crosshair and a dot follow the nearest point. The crosshair is `"dashed"` by default; choose `"solid"`, `"gradient"` or `false`. There's no tooltip on purpose. Use `useSparklineHover` to read the hovered `datum` and show it wherever you like, such as the big number on the card. Spread its `sparklineProps` onto several sparklines and their crosshairs move together.
