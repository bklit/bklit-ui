`ComposedChart` now composes every time-series mark. Alongside `Line`, `Area` and `SeriesBar`, you can drop in `Scatter`, `Candlestick` and `ProfitLossLine`, even inside fragments, and they share the y-domain and the tooltip.

## Two axes that stay out of each other's way

Each series picks an axis with `yAxisId`. The new `yDomains` prop overrides any axis's domain, either with a fixed `[min, max]` or with a function of the automatic one. That's how a price and volume chart keeps the volume bars in the bottom quarter while the candles float above. `Candlestick` works outside `CandlestickChart` now, sized with `widthRatio`, and its axis floats around the wicks instead of starting at zero.

## Loading and transitions

Composed charts take the same lifecycle props as `LineChart`: `status` and `loadingLabel` for the loading state, `xDomain`, and the animated y-domain when data changes.

## Gallery, Studio and recipes

The [composed chart gallery](/charts/composed-chart) has a new featured chart and six mixed-series variants, from price and volume to forecast bands and P&L with trades. In Studio, each series gets a **Type** control (bar, line, area, area and line, or scatter), plus **Stacked** and **Gap**. A new **Combos** section in [Recipes](/recipes?category=combos) collects four ready-made mixes.

See the [composed chart docs](/docs/components/composed-chart).
