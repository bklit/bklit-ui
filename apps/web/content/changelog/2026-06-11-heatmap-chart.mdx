`HeatmapChart` is here. Cells animate smoothly between the loading and ready states, and every level from Less to More shares one style — a base color plus an optional pattern, used by both the cells and the legend.

In Studio, the levels live in collapsible panes with the same pattern controls as `Background`.

The heatmap is in the docs, the gallery and the shadcn registry. See the [heatmap chart docs](/docs/components/heatmap-chart).
