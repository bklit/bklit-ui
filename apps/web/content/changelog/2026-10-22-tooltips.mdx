Tooltips are the part of a chart people touch most, so this week they got the most attention.

## A panel that matches your app

The panel now looks like the popovers in shadcn and coss ui: a rounded frame with a hairline border, a soft shadow, and a header row separated from the values. Series get small colour dots, labels are muted, and values are right-aligned in tabular figures so columns of numbers line up. Its colours come from your `popover` tokens, so it follows your theme without extra setup. Building custom tooltip content? The new `TooltipHeader` gives you the same header.

## Hover dots with a halo

The default hover dot is now a solid core inside a soft halo of the same colour. Both spring open when you hover and follow the tooltip as you move. Tune them with `dotHaloScale` and `dotHaloOpacity`.

## Motion that feels connected

- **Every tooltip fades out** when the pointer leaves, instead of vanishing. Pass `exit={false}` for the old behaviour.
- **Heatmap tooltips spring** from cell to cell like the other charts instead of snapping.
- **Jittered scatter tooltips** sit beside the point you are on and spring to the other side when they run out of room, rather than pinning to the top of the plot.
- **Reduced motion is respected**: the panel jumps into place and keeps only a short fade.

## Bar charts lose the crosshair

A vertical crosshair line helps on a line chart and gets in the way on a bar chart. Bar charts now hide it by default; pass `showCrosshair` to bring it back.

See the [Tooltip docs](/docs/utility/tooltip) for every option.
