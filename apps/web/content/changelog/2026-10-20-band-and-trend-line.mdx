Two new layers for showing what's around and underneath your data.

## Band

`Band` fills the space between an upper and a lower value — confidence intervals, min–max ranges, forecasts. Fill it solid, with a gradient or with a pattern, style each edge on its own, and fade any of its four sides. It draws in with the line it sits behind.

Bands also work on their own as range areas. The tooltip shows each band's low–high range with a dot on both edges, and the chart's scale fits the bands even when there's no line. For finance charts, `withBollingerBands` works the bands out for you.

## TrendLine

![A stepped line with a straight line running through it](/changelog/2026-10-20-trend-line.svg)

`TrendLine` draws the straight line of best fit through a series, so the direction of noisy data is obvious at a glance. Make it solid, dashed or a gradient, with optional faded ends. It works in line, area and composed charts, and in bar charts in either orientation.

Both are layers in Studio. See the [Band](/docs/utility/band) and [TrendLine](/docs/utility/trend-line) docs.
