The date pill that rides along the x-axis under the crosshair is one of the most-seen parts of a Bklit chart. You can now style it, replace it, make it glass, or turn it off and let the axis labels do the work.

## Style it or bring your own

`datePillClassName` merges your Tailwind classes over the default surface, and `datePillStyle` takes inline styles for anything Tailwind can't express. Set `datePillAnimation="static"` to swap the rolling label for a plain one. To replace the pill completely, pass a `datePill` render function: it receives the label, index and point, and your element is still positioned under the crosshair.

## Liquid glass and velocity swell

`datePillVariant="glass"` turns the dark capsule into liquid glass that bends the chart behind it at the rim. Refraction works in Chromium; Safari and Firefox get a frosted blur. Add `datePillVelocity` and the pill swells and stretches as you sweep across the chart, then springs back when you stop. It runs on motion values, so pointer moves don't re-render React, and it's off under reduced motion.

## Axis highlight

`XAxis` has a new `hoverEffect`. With `"highlight"`, labels near the crosshair change colour instead of fading out. Tune it with `activeLabelColor`, `nearbyLabelColor` and `highlightRadius`. The default, `"auto"`, fades labels while a date pill is shown and highlights them when it isn't.

## Breaking changes

Charts with `showDatePill={false}` now highlight axis labels instead of fading them. Set `hoverEffect="fade"` on `XAxis` to keep the old behaviour.

Two new recipes show it off: [Glass date pill](/recipes#glass-date-pill) and [Axis spotlight](/recipes#axis-spotlight). See the [tooltip docs](/docs/utility/tooltip) and [X axis docs](/docs/utility/axis/x-axis).
